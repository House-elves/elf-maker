# What's new

What changed for you in each version of Elf Maker, newest first. The desk shows the parts that are new when it offers you an
update.

Elf Maker comes with the desk and updates with it, so its changes arrive with a desk update.

## 0.2.0

- Paste a screenshot when you ask for a change: up to 4, pasted or dropped into "What should change?". The agent sees them with your words.
- Every elf you can change has Change it in its settings, which opens it here.

## 0.1.0

- Makes a new elf from what you tell it, and changes it when you ask. You read each version before it's published.
- Make it public: a private elf you published can be made public, after a check of everything in its history.
- Move it into House-elves: share your public elf with everyone by moving its repo in, from Elf Maker.
- Each version it builds says what's new in plain words, for the desk to show with the update.
